Sample photos for Keepers

The photos in this folder are frames from the HDR+ Burst Photography Dataset
by Google (https://hdrplusdata.org), licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes made for Keepers: the raw DNG burst frames were developed to JPEG,
rotated upright, and resized to 1600 pixels on the long edge. No location data
is included. These adapted images are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0
license.

Dataset reference: Samuel W. Hasinoff, Dillon Sharlet, Ryan Geiss, Andrew
Adams, Jonathan T. Barron, Florian Kainz, Jiawen Chen, and Marc Levoy. "Burst
photography for high dynamic range and low-light imaging on mobile cameras."
ACM Transactions on Graphics (SIGGRAPH Asia 2016).

The three screenshots (IMG_2041.PNG, IMG_2042.PNG and
Screenshot_20260614-171503.png) were made for Keepers to show how it sets
screenshots aside. They are MIT licensed with the rest of the project.
